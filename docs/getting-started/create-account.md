# Create your account

You need a k4cart account before you can create a store.

![Sign-in screen on mobile](/img/screenshots/sign-in.png)

## Sign up

1. Open [k4cart.com](https://k4cart.com) on your phone.
2. Tap **Sign up** (or go to [k4cart.com/sign-up](https://k4cart.com/sign-up)).
3. Enter your name, email address, and a password.
4. Submit the form.

![Sign-up screen on mobile](/img/screenshots/sign-up.png)

:::tip
Use the email address you check regularly. Order and account messages may go there, and you will need it to verify your account.
:::

## Verify your email

After sign-up, k4cart asks you to verify your email:

1. Open the verification message from k4cart in your inbox.
2. Tap the verification link (or enter the code if the app asks for one).
3. Return to k4cart and continue.

If you do not see the email, check spam or promotions. You can request a new verification message from the verify screen.

## Sign in later

1. Go to [k4cart.com/sign-in](https://k4cart.com/sign-in).
2. Enter your email and password.
3. You land in **Manage stores**, where you can open or create a store.
