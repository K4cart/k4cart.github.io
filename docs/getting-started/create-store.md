# Create your first store

A **store** is your online shop — products, orders, payments, and storefront all belong to it.

![Store overview after creation](/img/screenshots/overview-home.png)

## Open the create form

1. Sign in to k4cart.
2. If you have no stores yet, you will be prompted to create one.
3. From **All stores**, tap **Create store** (or the similar button on the page).

## Fill in store details

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|----------------|
| **Store name** | The name customers will see (e.g. *Kira Studio*). |
| **Description** | Optional short blurb about what you sell. |
| **Subdomain** | Your free web address. Only lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens. At least 3 characters. |

Your storefront URL becomes:

```text
https://your-subdomain.k4cart.com
```

k4cart suggests a subdomain from your store name. You can edit it. Availability is checked as you type — wait for confirmation that the name is free.

:::warning Subdomain rules
- Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only  
- Cannot start or end with a hyphen  
- Some names are reserved and cannot be used  
:::

## After creation

You open the store **dashboard** for that store. Your plan may limit how many stores you can create (Launch and Growth: **1 store**; Scale: **up to 3**). See [Plans & limits](../plans/overview.md).
