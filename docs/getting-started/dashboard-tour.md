# Dashboard tour

The **merchant dashboard** is where you manage your store. k4cart is designed for phone use first — most actions sit in the bottom navigation or a **More** menu.

![Store overview on mobile](/img/screenshots/overview-home.png)

## Bottom navigation (main tabs)

| Tab | What it is for |
|-----|----------------|
| **Home** | Overview: sales snapshot, open orders, and the go-live checklist until setup is done. |
| **Orders** | Customer orders — search, filter, and update status. |
| **Products** | Your catalogue — add, edit, stock, and publish products. |
| **Settings** | Store details, appearance, domains, payments, shipping, policies, and billing. |

## More menu

Open **More** (or the menu icon) for:

- **Carts** — checkouts that have not finished payment
- **Analytics** — visitors, orders, and revenue
- **Categories** — group products for browsing filters
- **Collections** — curated product groups (e.g. "New arrivals") that can appear in storefront navigation

## Store header

At the top of the store menu you will usually see:

- Your **store name**
- A link back to **All stores** if you manage more than one store
- **View store** — opens your public storefront in a new tab so you can shop it like a customer

## Home overview

When setup is incomplete, Home shows **Get [store] ready to sell** with five steps. When setup is complete, you still get:

- Revenue this month
- Orders this week
- Open orders that need attention
- Out-of-stock product count
- Checkouts awaiting payment
- A short revenue chart and recent open orders
