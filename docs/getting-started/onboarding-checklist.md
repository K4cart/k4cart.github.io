# Onboarding checklist

Until your store is ready to sell, **Home** shows a checklist: **Get [your store name] ready to sell**. Progress is shown as *X of 5 steps complete*.

Each incomplete step is tappable and opens the right screen.

## The five steps

| # | Step | Why it matters | Guide |
|---|------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | **Add your first product** | Customers need something to browse and buy. | [Add product](../go-live/add-product.md) |
| 2 | **Connect a payment method** | Customers pay you through your Razorpay account. | [Connect payments](../go-live/connect-payments.md) |
| 3 | **Set up shipping** | Delivery charges appear at checkout. | [Set up shipping](../go-live/setup-shipping.md) |
| 4 | **Add business & legal details** | Powers Terms, Privacy, Refund, and Contact pages. | [Business details](../go-live/business-details.md) |
| 5 | **Brand your storefront** | Logo makes your shop look professional. | [Brand storefront](../go-live/brand-storefront.md) |

## When the checklist disappears

After all five steps are done, the checklist is hidden and Home focuses on sales and orders. You can still change any of these later under **Products** and **Settings**.

## Tip for a smooth launch

Do the steps in order. Adding a product first lets you preview the storefront while you finish payments and shipping.
