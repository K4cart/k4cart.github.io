# 1. Add your first product

**Where:** **Products** → **Add product** (or **Add your first product** on Home)

![New product form on mobile](/img/screenshots/products-new.png)

Products are what customers browse and purchase on your storefront.

## Quick path (simple product)

1. Open **Products**.  
2. Tap **Add product** (or **New**).  
3. Enter a clear **Name** (e.g. *Silk Scarf – Midnight Blue*).  
4. Optionally add a short **Description**.  
5. Upload one or more **photos** (clear, well-lit images sell better).  
6. Set **Selling price** in ₹.  
7. Optionally set **MRP** (must be higher than selling price if you use it).  
8. Set **Stock** (how many you can sell).  
9. Optionally set a **SKU** (your internal code).  
10. Leave the product **Active** so it appears on the storefront.  
11. Save.

You can refine categories and variants later — see the [full products guide](../catalog/products.md).

## Tips

- Use rupee amounts customers recognise (e.g. `1299` for ₹1,299).  
- At least one good photo makes a large difference on mobile.  
- Keep stock accurate so you do not oversell.  
- Launch plan allows **up to 100 products** per store; Growth and Scale allow unlimited.

## Checklist complete when

Your store has **at least one product**.
