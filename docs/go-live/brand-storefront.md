# 5. Brand your storefront

**Where:** **Settings → Appearance** (or **Brand your storefront** on Home)

![Appearance settings on mobile](/img/screenshots/settings-appearance.png)

A logo helps customers recognise you on a phone-sized header.

## Add your logo

1. Open **Settings → Appearance**.  
2. Under **Store logo**, upload an image (transparent background works well; max about 5 MB; images are compressed automatically).  
3. Save if prompted.

### Hide store name when logo is shown

If your logo already includes your brand name (a wordmark), turn on **Hide store name when logo is shown** so the header does not repeat the name twice.

## Homepage banners (optional but recommended)

Still under **Appearance**, you can add **banner images** for a carousel on the storefront home page. Use wide, mobile-friendly creatives (sales, new arrivals, festival offers).

## Preview

Tap **View store** in the dashboard menu to open your public shop and check logo and banners on your phone.

## Checklist complete when

Your store has a **logo** uploaded.

## You are ready

When all five go-live steps are done, share your storefront link and start fulfilling [orders](../orders/managing-orders.md).

For more branding options later, see [Appearance settings](../settings/appearance.md).
