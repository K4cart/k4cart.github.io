# 4. Business & legal details

**Where:** **Settings → Policies** (or **Add business & legal details** on Home)

![Policies settings on mobile](/img/screenshots/settings-policies.png)

These details power the **Terms**, **Privacy**, **Refund**, **Shipping**, and **Contact** information customers see on your storefront.

## Required for the checklist

Fill at least:

- **Business name**  
- **Support email**  

## Recommended fields

| Field | Why |
|-------|-----|
| **Support phone** | So customers can reach you easily |
| **Address** | Builds trust and may be needed for policies |
| **GSTIN** | If you have a GST number, add it for invoices and trust |

## How legal pages work

k4cart builds clear default policy text from your business details and shipping settings. You can optionally **override** Terms, Privacy, Refund, or Shipping text with your own wording if you prefer.

1. Open **Settings → Policies**.  
2. Complete business name, support email, and other fields.  
3. Review the default page previews if shown.  
4. Only use override boxes if you need custom legal wording.  
5. Save.

:::info
The Home checklist marks this step done when **business name** and **support email** are both filled.
:::
