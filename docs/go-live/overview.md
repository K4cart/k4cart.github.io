# Go live overview

Use this path to take a new store from empty to ready for customers. It matches the checklist on **Home**.

![Merchant dashboard overview on mobile](/img/screenshots/overview-home.png)

## Checklist

1. [Add your first product](./add-product.md)  
2. [Connect payments (Razorpay)](./connect-payments.md)  
3. [Set up shipping](./setup-shipping.md)  
4. [Add business & legal details](./business-details.md)  
5. [Brand your storefront](./brand-storefront.md)  

## Before you share your link

Confirm:

- At least one **active** product with a price and stock  
- Payments show as connected under **Settings → Payments**  
- Shipping is **enabled** with a rate you are happy with  
- Support email and business name are filled under **Settings → Policies**  
- Logo (or at least a clear store name) looks good on **View store**  

Then share your storefront URL (e.g. `https://yourstore.k4cart.com`) on WhatsApp, Instagram, or your packaging.
