# 3. Set up shipping

**Where:** **Settings → Shipping** (or **Set up shipping** on Home)

![Shipping settings on mobile](/img/screenshots/settings-shipping.png)

Shipping settings control what customers pay for delivery at checkout.

## Configure delivery pricing

1. Open **Settings → Shipping**.  
2. Turn **shipping on** (enabled).  
3. Enter a **flat rate** in ₹ — the delivery fee for orders (you can use `0` for free delivery always).  
4. Optionally set a **free shipping threshold** — when the cart reaches this amount, delivery becomes free (e.g. free shipping above ₹999).  
5. Save.

## Examples

| Goal | Flat rate | Free threshold |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Always ₹50 delivery | `50` | leave empty |
| Free delivery over ₹1,500 | `80` | `1500` |
| Free delivery for everyone | `0` | not needed |

## Checklist complete when

Shipping is **enabled** for the store.
