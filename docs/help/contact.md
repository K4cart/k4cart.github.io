# Contact support

## Email

| Need | Address |
|------|---------|
| Account, store, billing help | [support@k4cart.com](mailto:support@k4cart.com) |
| Partnerships & business | [hello@k4cart.com](mailto:hello@k4cart.com) |

## Support hours

- **Monday to Saturday**  
- **10:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST**  
- Typical response: **within 1–2 business days**

## Before you write

Include:

1. Your account email  
2. Store name or subdomain  
3. What you were trying to do  
4. Screenshots from your phone (hide Razorpay secrets and customer private data)  

More options: [k4cart.com/contact](https://k4cart.com/contact).
