# Welcome to k4cart Help

**k4cart** helps you open an online store, list products, take payments with your own Razorpay account, and fulfil orders — without needing a developer.

![Customer storefront on mobile](/img/screenshots/storefront-home.png)

These guides are written for **merchants** (boutique owners, jewellery sellers, fashion brands, and similar). You will not need technical knowledge to follow them.

## What you can do with k4cart

- Create a store on a free address like `yourstore.k4cart.com` (and a custom domain on higher plans)
- Add products with photos, prices in ₹, stock, and size/colour variants
- Organise your catalogue with categories and collections
- Accept customer payments that go **directly to your Razorpay account**
- Set delivery charges and free-shipping thresholds
- Publish Terms, Privacy, Refund, and Contact pages from your business details
- Process orders from Confirmed through to Delivered

## Path to your first sale

1. [Create your account](./getting-started/create-account.md)
2. [Create your store](./getting-started/create-store.md)
3. Complete the [go-live checklist](./go-live/overview.md) (products, payments, shipping, legal details, branding)
4. Share your storefront link and fulfil [orders](./orders/managing-orders.md)

## Plans at a glance

| Plan | From | Best for |
|------|------|----------|
| **Launch** | ₹299/month | First store, up to 100 products |
| **Growth** | ₹799/month | Unlimited products, custom domain |
| **Scale** | ₹1,499/month | Up to 3 stores |

See [Plans & limits](./plans/overview.md) for full details. New accounts typically include a **14-day trial**.
