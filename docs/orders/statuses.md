# Order statuses explained

Orders move through a simple fulfilment path. Keeping status up to date helps you stay organised and matches what customers expect after checkout.

## Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Confirmed** | Payment succeeded; order is new and waiting for you to act. |
| **Processing** | You are preparing the order (picking, packing, quality check). |
| **Shipped** | Order has left you / is with the courier or in transit. |
| **Delivered** | Customer has received the order. |
| **Cancelled** | Order will not be fulfilled. |

## Normal flow

```text
Confirmed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered
```

## Cancellation

You can cancel from **Confirmed** or **Processing**. After shipping, coordinate carefully with the customer and your courier; use Razorpay for any refund process as needed.

## Tips

- Update status the same day you ship so nothing sits as “open” on Home.  
- Use filters on the Orders list to focus on one status at a time during busy periods.
