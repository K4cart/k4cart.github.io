# Plans & limits

k4cart charges a **fixed subscription**. It does **not** take a percentage of your sales. Customer payments go to your Razorpay account.

Prices below are current list prices; taxes may apply separately when required.

## Compare plans

| | **Launch** | **Growth** | **Scale** |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Monthly** | ₹299 | ₹799 | ₹1,499 |
| **Annual** | ₹2,999 | ₹7,999 | ₹14,999 |
| Stores | 1 | 1 | Up to 3 |
| Products | Up to 100 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Storefront | Free subdomain | Custom domain | Custom domain |
| Customer payments | Your Razorpay account | Your Razorpay account | Your Razorpay account |
| Support | Email | Email | Priority |
| Trial | 14 days | 14 days | 14 days |

## When to upgrade

**Launch → Growth** when you need:

- More than 100 products  
- Your own domain (e.g. `www.yourbrand.com`)  

**Growth → Scale** when you need:

- More than one store (up to 3)  
- Priority support  

## Manage billing

Open **Settings → Billing** in the dashboard. Details: [Billing & subscription](../settings/billing.md).
