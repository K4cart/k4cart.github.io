# Plans & limits

k4cart has **one plan**. Every merchant gets the same features and limits. k4cart charges a **fixed subscription** and does **not** take a percentage of your sales. Customer payments go to your Razorpay account.

![Launch plan on k4cart.com](/img/screenshots/marketing-pricing.png)

Prices below are what you see on [k4cart.com](https://k4cart.com/#pricing). A **Launch offer** shows ₹299/month with the regular ₹499/month price struck through. Taxes, if applicable, may be charged separately.

## Launch plan

| | **Launch** |
|---|---|
| **Monthly** | ~~₹499~~ **₹299** (launch offer) |
| Stores | 1 |
| Products | Unlimited |
| Storefront | Free subdomain or custom domain |
| Analytics and cart insights | Included |
| Customer payments | Your Razorpay account |
| Support | Email |
| Trial | 14 days |

Also included:

- No k4cart platform transaction fee (payment gateway charges still apply)
- Connect your Razorpay account from **Settings → Payments**

## Limits

- **1 store** per account. You cannot create a second store on this plan.
- **Unlimited products** in that store.
- Custom domains are included — you do not need a higher plan to use `www.yourbrand.com`.

## Trial

New accounts start with a **14-day trial**. You can try every feature without adding a payment method. After the trial, you need an active subscription to keep selling.

Online checkout for the k4cart subscription itself may not be available yet. If billing is not open on **Settings → Billing**, contact [support@k4cart.com](mailto:support@k4cart.com).

## Manage billing

Open **Settings → Billing** in the dashboard. Details: [Billing & subscription](../settings/billing.md).

Marketing pricing page: [k4cart.com/pricing](https://k4cart.com/pricing) (this redirects to the pricing section on the home page).
