# Billing & subscription

**Where:** **Settings → Billing**

![Billing on mobile](/img/screenshots/settings-billing.png)

This page is about your **k4cart subscription** (the fee you pay k4cart), not customer order payments.

## What you will see

- Current **plan** (Launch, Growth, or Scale)  
- **Status** such as free trial, active, payment due, or lapsed  
- Relevant dates (trial end, next billing period when available)  
- Plan comparison cards with monthly or annual pricing  

## Trial

New merchants typically start with a **14-day trial**. Use the trial to complete go-live and take test orders with live or test payment setup as appropriate.

## If the subscription lapses

When a subscription is no longer active, the store may become **read-only** so you do not lose data, but selling and edits can be restricted until billing is resolved. Check this page and contact support if you need help restoring access.
