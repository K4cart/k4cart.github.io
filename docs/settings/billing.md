# Billing & subscription

**Where:** **Settings → Billing**

![Billing on mobile](/img/screenshots/settings-billing.png)

This page is about your **k4cart subscription** (the fee you pay k4cart), not customer order payments.

## What you will see

- Current **plan** — **Launch** (the only plan)
- **Status** such as free trial, active, payment due, or lapsed
- Relevant dates (trial end, or next billing date when available)
- How many **stores** you are using (1 of 1 on Launch)
- The Launch plan card with the **launch-offer** price (₹299/month, regular ₹499/month struck through) and what is included

There is no monthly/annual toggle and no upgrade grid — every merchant is on Launch.

## Trial

New merchants typically start with a **14-day trial**. Use the trial to complete go-live and take test orders with live or test payment setup as appropriate. You do not need a payment method to start the trial.

## If the subscription lapses

When a subscription is no longer active, the store may become **read-only** so you do not lose data, but selling and edits can be restricted until billing is resolved. Check this page and contact support if you need help restoring access.
