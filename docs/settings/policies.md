# Policies & legal pages

**Where:** **Settings → Policies**

![Policies on mobile](/img/screenshots/settings-policies.png)

## Business details

Enter:

- Business name  
- Support email (required for a complete setup)  
- Support phone  
- Address  
- GSTIN (if applicable)  

These feed customer-facing legal and contact information.

## Default policy text

k4cart generates sensible default **Terms**, **Privacy**, **Refund**, and **Shipping** policy content from your details. Review it after you save.

## Custom wording (optional)

If your lawyer or brand needs different wording, use the override fields for each policy type. Leave them blank to keep the defaults.

First-time setup: [Business & legal details](../go-live/business-details.md).
