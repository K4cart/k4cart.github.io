# Shipping

**Where:** **Settings → Shipping**

![Shipping on mobile](/img/screenshots/settings-shipping.png)

## Settings

| Setting | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Enabled** | When on, delivery pricing is applied at checkout. |
| **Flat rate** | Delivery fee in ₹ for orders (use `0` for free delivery). |
| **Free shipping threshold** | Optional cart total above which delivery is free. |

Save after changes. Customers see updated rates on the next checkout.

First-time setup: [Set up shipping](../go-live/setup-shipping.md).
